Game tools, trackers and calculators

BuffForge is a free collection of game tools, trackers and calculators. Every number cites a public source and a review date. There is no login and no advertising. Tools are organized by game: open a game to find its trackers, checkers and (when we have sourced figures) guides. BuffForge is an independent fan project, not affiliated with any publisher. Written guides are added only when we can source the numbers — today the live work is the tools.

How we check every number

  1. Public sources onlyNumbers come from official notes, official wikis, and public APIs.
  2. Versioned and datedEvery tool shows its data version, sources, and review date.
  3. Tell us when we are wrongIf we get a number wrong, tell us. We correct it.

Frequently asked questions

What is BuffForge?

Do I need an account?

No.

Is BuffForge official?

No. BuffForge is an independent fan project, not affiliated with any publisher.
