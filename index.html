Written guides are added only when we can source the numbers — today the live work is the tools.

The method

How we check every number

  1. Public sources onlyNumbers come from official notes, official or well-sourced wikis, and public APIs. We never invent odds.
  2. Versioned and datedEvery tool shows its data version, sources, and review date. Early-access figures can change.
  3. Tell us when we are wrongIf we get a number wrong, tell us. We correct it.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

I play MMOs — which tools are actually useful?

The ones that track what resets. In AION 2, daily duties reset at 07:00 UTC and weekly duties every Wednesday — miss a reset and that day is gone. The AION 2 tracker on this site lists your duties and counts down to each reset, so you can see what is still open before you log off.

I play idle games — how do I stop wasting items?

Check the odds before you commit. In Desktop Pension Squad, nine items go into one fusion and the game never shows the odds. Our calculator uses the real odds from the game client (v1.0.2, build 26611958) and compares fusing against selling, so you can see which one pays off before you spend the batch.

I play single-player games — what should I check before launch?

Four things: disk space, unlock time, edition, and console. For GTA 6 (November 19, 2026) this site has a storage check (at least 212 GB), a countdown by store region, a Standard vs Ultimate comparison ($79.99 vs $99.99), and a console compatibility check — all from official facts only.

What about FPS games?

The tools FPS players ask for most are a sensitivity converter, a crosshair overlay, and an FPS/ping monitor — they keep your aim consistent and tell you whether a bad round is you or your connection. BuffForge does not list one yet: we only add tools whose numbers can be sourced and checked.

How do you check numbers?

Public sources only. Every tool shows its data version, sources, and review date. Corrections are welcome — see About.
