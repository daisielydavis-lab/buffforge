Can My Console Run GTA 6?

Pick your device. GTA 6 has been available since 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 (including Pro) and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One has no native version. Cloud play is live since 19 Nov 2026: a separate $79.99 purchase, not in Game Pass, and it streams to Xbox consoles only — not to PC, phones, or TVs. PC is not announced.
