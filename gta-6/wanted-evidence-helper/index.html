GTA 6 Wanted Evidence Helper — what do the cops know about you?

GTA 6 brings back six wanted stars, and this time the police act on evidence — six kinds of it. Tick what they have on you and get a plan to go cold.

Pre-launch — based on pre-release previews

The heat scale is the tool’s own scale, not an in-game number. Star values and fade speed are not confirmed before launch; this page will be verified against the live game after Nov 19, 2026.

How the evidence system works

  • Six stars are back. GTA 5 had five; GTA 6 has six.
  • Stars follow evidence, not just noise. What the police know about you changes how hard they come — and that knowledge comes in six types below.
  • Witnesses matter. The police act on what people actually saw and reported.
  • Cash is at risk. Cash on hand is lost if you die or get arrested; money in the joint bank account is safe.
  • Criminal Profile is a separate system. It tracks patterns in how you commit crimes. It is not a good-or-evil morality meter.
