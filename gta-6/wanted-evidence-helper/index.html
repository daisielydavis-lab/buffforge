GTA 6 Wanted & Heat Guide

How the GTA 6 Wanted Level system works

Unlike a simple five-star chase, GTA VI uses up to six wanted stars alongside a recognition-based Heat system. Police can react to reports from witnesses and alarms, and the icons beneath your stars represent identifying information. Escaping the immediate pursuit and reducing recognition are related, but separate tasks. This tool helps you plan both, based on manually entered game state.

GTA 6 Heat icons explained

◉ Appearance / Face

Police have your physical description. Changing accessories or hairstyle, or removing a mask after escaping sight, can alter recognition.

♧ Outfit

Police know the clothes you wore. Change the full outfit, not just one small accessory.

▣ Vehicle

The vehicle has been identified. Switching vehicles or respraying may help; tracker restrictions can apply.

⌁ Visible weapon

Visible weapons attract attention. Holster a concealable weapon or stow a long gun.

♧♧ Partner

Jason and Lucia were seen together. Separating the pair can reduce that particular identifier.

▤ CCTV

Ordinary locally stored footage may be destructible. Check the location before assuming the recording is gone.

☁ Cloud CCTV

Cloud-stored evidence cannot be cleared by destroying a local recorder. Reduce other identifiers and exit the area.

Vehicle trackers: yellow vs red. A standard yellow tracker only alerts after the car has come to a complete stop for a few seconds, and registering the car at a Pay ‘n’ Spray — from Chapter 3 — removes it for good. An advanced red tracker alerts after about five seconds below the speed limit, and Pay ‘n’ Spray usually refuses the car until the advanced tracker service is unlocked through Randy’s vehicle-theft line.

Blood stains and dye packs. They make you easier to recognise — a shower at a safehouse clears them.

Six stars can't be escaped. At six stars the military joins the manhunt and the whole map stays hot. No disguise, vehicle swap or hiding spot clears it — the pursuit only ends if you're Wasted or Busted.

Escape strategy: pursue, evade, then clear Heat

First, break line of sight. Use available cover and a route that avoids the search area. Second, check the Heat icons. If police know your clothes, car or face, address each independently. Third, avoid re-entering the active Heat zone. A pursuit can end while the area remains hot, making renewed detection possible.

Mission objectives can temporarily override ordinary free-roam behavior. If your icon will not clear after an otherwise valid action, check the mission objective and whether police still have a direct visual on you. The tool does not guarantee that a specific icon will disappear.

Frequently asked questions

How do I lose cops in GTA 6?

+

Break their line of sight, leave the active search area, then reduce the identity details shown by your red Heat icons. Choose your status and known evidence above for an actionable checklist.

What do the red icons under wanted stars mean?

+

They are recognition or evidence indicators: your appearance, outfit, car, visible weapon, partner and CCTV records. Cloud-stored CCTV may be represented separately.

Why is the outfit icon still red after changing clothes?

+

Ensure you changed the full outfit rather than only an accessory. Some missions may control when an icon clears, and other identifiers remain independent. This is not proof of a bug by itself.

Can changing my car remove all Heat?

+

No. Vehicle evidence is separate from appearance, outfit and CCTV. Some tracked vehicles can also have special restrictions.

Can I delete cloud CCTV evidence?

+

Published guides distinguish cloud CCTV from on-site footage: the cloud recording cannot be erased by destroying a local device. Focus on escaping and addressing other recognition details.

Can you escape a 6-star wanted level?

+

No. At six stars the military joins the manhunt and the wanted level can't be cleared — breaking line of sight or changing your look won't end it. The pursuit only ends if you're Wasted or Busted.

Is this connected to my GTA 6 game?

+

No. This is an offline-in-the-browser decision helper. It does not read live HUD data, modify game files, remove police Heat or access Rockstar accounts.
