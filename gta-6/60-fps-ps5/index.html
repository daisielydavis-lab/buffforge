GTA 6 at 60 FPS on PS5? Free Remote Play guide

GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on PS5 and PS5 Pro, with no 60 mode. Free SmoovPlay on a Windows PC can draw extra frames from Remote Play, so the PC monitor looks smoother. The game stays at 30. This is unproven on a live PS5 copy of GTA 6.

Download SmoovPlay only from its GitHub Releases. The Windows installer is an early unsigned build.

You need a PC

This can help

  • Windows 10 or 11, and an NVIDIA RTX 30, 40, or 50 card. That range is marked Tested.
  • You play on the PC monitor, with the controller paired to the PC.
  • You can live with extra delay.

This will not help

  • You only have a PS5 and a TV.
  • You want the game itself to run at 60.
  • You expected AMD, Intel, Xbox, or a capture card. Those are Experimental.

What you need

Labels below are the project's own, from the SmoovPlay README.

PCWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
GraphicsNVIDIA RTX 30, 40, or 50 — Tested. AMD and Intel — Experimental.
ConsoleA PS5 with Remote Play on — Tested. An Xbox app or a capture card — Experimental.
NetworkEthernet, or 5 GHz or 6 GHz Wi-Fi.
Screen60 Hz or faster. You watch this monitor, not the TV on the PS5.

Set it up

  1. Turn on Remote PlayOn the PS5, open Settings → System → Remote Play and turn on Enable Remote Play. Keep the PS5 and the PC on the same home network.
  2. Download the installerOn the PC, download SmoovPlay-Setup.exe from the official GitHub Releases only.
  3. Install itRun that file and wait until setup finishes. The project says this takes about 10 to 20 minutes.
  4. Connect the PS5In the app, follow Connect your PS5, sign in to PSN there, and register the console. chiaki-ng only carries the Remote Play stream; if it asks for a code, the PS5 shows one at Remote Play → Link Device.
  5. Pair the controllerConnect the controller to the PC. Play looking at the PC monitor.
  6. Press Play, then pick 60Press Play. Press M and set the frame rate to 60 on a 60 Hz screen.

Keys

The controller plays the game. These keys run SmoovPlay, from the README.

MSettings while you play, including the frame rate.
FFullscreen.
IExtra frames off and on.
Esc or QStop.

Smoother, not a 60 FPS game

The extra frames are drawn on the PC after the stream arrives. The console does not start rendering at 60.

The game stays at its native 30, so the controls do not become a 60 FPS game. The stream and the extra frames add delay.

HUD text, fast camera turns, and thin objects that cross can wobble. The picture arrives at 1080p, a little softer than HDMI from the console.

More settings

Press Auto-detect on the Settings page if you change monitors. If a 30 FPS game looks uneven, set Settings → Smoothness → Game frame rate to 30 fps.

On a faster screen the project also lists 90, and up to 120. It says no live 120 measurement is published.

While SmoovPlay is open, leave NVIDIA Smooth Motion, AMD Fluid Motion Frames, and Lossless Scaling off.

Check the download

The release used for this page is v0.3.8, 19 November 2026. To confirm the file, compare its SHA-256 with the hash on that Releases page. In PowerShell: Get-FileHash on the installer.

Leave Windows protection as it is. Do not type a PSN password into any site except the sign-in the app opens.

If the picture hitches

Plug the PS5 and the PC into the router. Wi-Fi can bunch frames.

If it still fails, press Q, then Help → Copy diagnostics, and send that to a SmoovPlay issue. Not to this site.

Questions

Why is GTA 6 only 30 FPS on PS5?

Digital Foundry measured a 30 FPS target on PS5 and PS5 Pro, and found no 40 or 60 mode. PS5 Pro holds that 30 more steadily. It is not a 60 mode.

Is GTA 6 locked to 30 FPS?

On current consoles, yes. The same test found no 40 or 60 option on PS5, PS5 Pro, or Xbox Series X and Series S.

Will GTA 6 run at 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

Not as a mode in the game. PS5 Pro uses the same 30 FPS target. This guide's 60 is extra pictures on a PC, and the game stays at 30.

Has this been shown on a real PS5 copy of GTA 6?

Not as a live recording of the game. SmoovPlay's GTA VI clips are Rockstar's trailer, An Extended Look, played as a simulated stream. The live PS5 recording they show is Gotham Knights.

I only have a PS5 and a TV. Does this help?

No. The extra frames are drawn on a Windows PC, and you watch them on that PC's monitor.
