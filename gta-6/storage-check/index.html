GTA 6 Storage Check

PlayStation still says to clear a minimum of 212 GB of free space. That is the store’s free-space line, not a Rockstar install size. Measured downloads are about 209.75 GB on PS5 and 209.86 GB on Xbox, installed at about 212 GB. Enter what your console shows as free.

On PS5: Settings → Storage. On Xbox: My games & apps → Manage → Free up space.

What 212 GB means

  • 212 GB is the minimum free space on the PlayStation game page. Rockstar has not published a separate final install size.
  • Preload already happened. PlayStation said digital preload for pre-orders started 10 Nov 2026 at local midnight. The game has been available since 19 Nov 2026.
  • Xbox: this page does not publish a Moscow preload clock. Use the time on your own Xbox store listing.
  • Patches: 1.002.000 and 1.002.001 were out before launch; 1.002.002 followed on 23 Nov 2026. Leave about 230 GB free so updates fit.

Source: PlayStation game page for Grand Theft Auto VI, “minimum of 212GB”, checked 25 Nov 2026. Download sizes are measured reports (about 209.75 GB on PS5, about 209.86 GB on Xbox), not an official Rockstar figure.
