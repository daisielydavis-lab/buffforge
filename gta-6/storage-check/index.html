GTA 6 Storage & Preload Check

GTA 6 needs a lot of disk. PlayStation's official support page lists at least 212 GB of free space. Check yours before preload day — not after the download fails.

On PS5: Settings → Storage. On Xbox: My games & apps → Manage → Free up space.

Preload: what is confirmed

  • PS5: digital preload starts at local midnight for your account's region. The exact moment depends on your store region, not on one global time.
  • Xbox: preload start time is shown in the Xbox store / console for your region. Check the store listing — we do not publish an unverified Moscow time.
  • 212 GB is the free space PlayStation lists as required. The final download size has not been separately confirmed by Rockstar.

Sources: PlayStation official support (GTA VI), checked Nov 13, 2026. Always confirm in-game / in the store.
