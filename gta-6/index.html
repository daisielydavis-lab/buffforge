This hub answers those from official pages: a storage check, the unlock time by store region, Standard vs Ultimate, and a compatibility check. Standard $79.99 and Ultimate $99.99 are the US store prices, confirmed after launch. Xbox One has no native version; cloud play is live since 19 Nov 2026 — a separate $79.99 purchase, not in Game Pass, streams to Xbox consoles only. There is no announced PC version.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How big is GTA 6?

PlayStation lists a minimum of 212 GB free. Measured downloads are about 209.75 GB on PS5 and 209.86 GB on Xbox, installed at about 212 GB. Leave about 230 GB free. Use the storage check.

When did GTA 6 come out?

19 Nov 2026, at local midnight in your store region. It is out. The launch-time page shows that midnight and the time since.

Standard or Ultimate — what is the difference?

Standard $79.99 and Ultimate $99.99 are the US store prices, confirmed after launch. Other regions differ. The pre-order window closed at the end of 19 Nov 2026. See the editions page for the PlayStation Ultimate list.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC?

There is no officially announced PC version, and no official PC system requirements. Do not trust spec tables you see online — they are not from Rockstar.

Can I play on PS4 or Xbox One?

PS4: no. Xbox One: no native version. Cloud play is live since 19 Nov 2026: a separate $79.99 purchase, not in Game Pass, streams to Xbox consoles only.
