Frequently asked questions

How big is GTA 6?

At least 212 GB of free space, according to PlayStation. Check your console with the storage check.

When does GTA 6 come out?

Nov 19, 2026, at local midnight in your store region — see the launch countdown for your time zone.

Standard or Ultimate — what is the difference?

Standard is $79.99 and Ultimate is $99.99 (US MSRP from Take-Two). See the editions comparison for what each one includes.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC?

There is no officially announced PC version, and no official PC system requirements. Do not trust spec tables you see online — they are not from Rockstar.

Can I play on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. GTA 6 launches on PS5 (including Pro) and Xbox Series X|S only.
