GTA 6 Launch Countdown

GTA 6 unlocks Nov 19, 2026 at local midnight — midnight in your store region's time zone. There is no single official Moscow unlock time: it depends on which region your account's store is in.

—

What is confirmed — and what is not

  • Confirmed: launch date Nov 19, 2026; PS5 (including Pro) and Xbox Series X|S; no PC version at launch (Rockstar).
  • Not a single global moment: because unlock is local midnight per region, the Moscow time differs by region. Any site quoting one Moscow unlock time for everyone is guessing.
  • Preload: PS5 digital preload starts at local midnight for your account's region. Xbox preload time is shown in the Xbox store.

Sources: Rockstar Games official newswire (launch date), PlayStation official support, checked Nov 13, 2026.
