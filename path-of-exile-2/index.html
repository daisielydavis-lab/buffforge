Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

When does Path of Exile 2 1.0 come out?

11 Dec 2026 (PST). The exact launch hour has not been announced. The game becomes free to play at launch.

Are these tools updated for 1.0?

Not yet. The class list, resistance penalties and campaign rewards reflect Early Access 0.5.5, reviewed 25 Nov 2026. Acts 5–6 and the new classes' final numbers are not presented as confirmed until they are verified after launch.

Which class should I start with?

Start with the combat pattern you enjoy, not a damage ranking. The Class Selector matches the eight Early Access classes by playstyle. Shadow and Duelist arrive with 1.0 and are not scored until their final mechanics are verified.

Do I need an account?

No. The tools run in your browser; the campaign checklist saves locally.
