AION 2 tools and guides

This is the BuffForge hub for AION 2 tools and guides on the global servers. The live tool is a daily and weekly tracker: tick duties off and watch the countdown to the 07:00 UTC daily reset and the Wednesday weekly reset. Reset times and any figures we publish cite public sources and a review date. Guides will appear here only when we can source them. BuffForge is an independent fan project, not affiliated with NC. No login, no ads.

Frequently asked questions

What time is the AION 2 daily reset?

07:00 UTC, every day.

When is the weekly reset?

Wednesday at 07:00 UTC.

Is there an official NC tracker?

No. NCSoft does not publish an official daily and weekly tracker — this one is built from public sources and community trackers, with a review date on the page.

Do I need an account?

No. The tracker runs in your browser and saves your checkmarks locally.
