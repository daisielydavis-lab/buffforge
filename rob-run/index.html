Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

When is the Federal Raid?

Seven days after that server opened. Servers launched on 4 Nov 2026 were raided on 11 Nov 2026 at 20:00 MSK (17:00 UTC). A server opened later keeps its own clock.

What is wiped, and what stays?

Plots, bases, weapons and cash are wiped and turned into jail time and authority. Cosmetics, your name, tutorial progress, settings and accumulated jail time stay. Source: Kefir’s Federal Raid FAQ.

Do the calculators know in-game prices?

No. You type the cash amounts. This site is not connected to the game.
