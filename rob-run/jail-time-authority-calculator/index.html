How ROB RUN Jail Time & Authority Work (2026 Guide)

In ROB RUN, there is no traditional XP bar. Every seven days, the Federal Raid wipes all plots, bases, weapons, vehicles, and cash on your server and converts that wealth into permanent Jail Time — which directly equals your Authority level.

The $500,000-Per-Year Conversion Formula & Caps

Based on community testing across Steam discussions and game-data wikis, every $500,000 in confiscated assets awards +1 year of Jail Time (rounded down), capped at 40 years per server (~$20,000,000 in assets). Following Kefir's November 17, 2026 patch, community trackers also list a 40 years per 24-hour daily limit and a 1,200-year overall account cap.

Note on the +1 year: some guides (robrun.info, Indiatimes) list a 1-year starting sentence on top of the $500K formula. This calculator follows the Steam breakdown thread and robrunwiki (no automatic +1 year), capped at 40 years per server.

Why Lenny's $1,000,000 Debt Is the Fastest Early Boost

Paying off Lenny's $1,000,000 debt awards the New Chapter skin plus a one-time +10 years of Jail Time that stacks on top of the 40-year server cap. At $100,000 per year, it is 5x more efficient than standard Federal Raid asset conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Jail Time calculated at the Federal Raid?

+

Players report 1 year of Jail Time per $500,000 of confiscated server assets, rounded down, up to 40 years ($20M) per server cycle.

Do I need to surrender to the Feds before the wipe?

+

No. Your server assets convert automatically during the 7-day Federal Raid wipe even while offline. Early Federal Surrender is mainly used to switch servers immediately. Check when your server wipes on the Federal Raid countdown.

What is the fastest solo route to farm Authority?

+

Without a plot, solo Store Runs sold to the Fence average ~$200,000/hr (~2.5 hrs/year). Once you own a plot, a maxed Grow Room can exceed $500,000/hr if defended against base raids.

Does getting arrested by a player cop give Jail Time?

+

No. Player police arrests confiscate your bag loot but grant 0 Jail Time. Only Federal Raid wipes, Federal Surrender, and Lenny's $1M debt add permanent Authority.

What is the highest Authority reward in ROB RUN?

+

On the prison Tattoo Artist “Trust No One” track, the final cosmetic reward is the legendary Silence Mask at 800 Authority. In Shipments, the DRM-14 Mk3 unlocks at 1,000 years.
