What is in here

What can you search in this wiki?

This search covers five heroes in the files. Geumeum is not on sale. Chopper is the Character Pack DLC. Also here: 40 active skill names, nine obtainable pets, 160 stage identifiers, 180 equipment tiers and the rules we have checked. Worn Compass stays hidden. Character pages and player-save access are not required.

How do active skills work?

Active skills activate automatically when their conditions are met. A hero can equip up to three. You can search by skill name, hero and trigger in the Skills category.

How many difficulties and stages are there?

The game has Normal, Torment, Nightmare and Hell; each includes four chapters with ten stages, for 160 stage identifiers in total. The tenth stage of each chapter is a boss stage. Individual enemy and drop tables are not included without source-checked values.

Does the wiki include every equipment item and passive skill?

No. All 180 equipment tier names and rarity ranges are indexed, but individual grade stats and the 65 passive skill details are not included. Rather than showing fabricated effects or drop probabilities, missing fields are clearly marked. This is a functioning search page but not a complete extracted game database.

Where does the game data come from?

The rows were taken from public reports of v1.0.3 (Steam build 26637932) on 15 Nov 2026. They were not extracted again from the current client. On 22 Nov 2026 DPSquad Wiki’s current data was v1.0.6, build 26804378. That page says stages, gear, boxes and the Workshop are unchanged, and that encrypted code still uses the v1.0.3 rule read. A few wording notes on this page were re-read that day. The wiki is a fan site, not official.

Why are there only nine pets?

Worn Compass is in the files and hidden until its pack is out. The v1.0.6 wiki page, checked 22 Nov 2026, still says nobody can unlock or equip it, so it is not one of the nine obtainable pets. Search still finds it, marked not obtainable.
